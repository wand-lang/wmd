Lesson 2: a lever that counts, with state and save

Go to the Lever Room: west from the Commons, then two. Pull the lever,
then /examine lever.

An object never changes. A verb gives back a Reply: actions for the
driver, and `next`, the object to use from now on. The lever keeps its
count in a record, State, and makes a new object over a new State:

  type State(pulls: Int = 0)

  next = Some (make State(pulls = s.pulls + 1))

`save` gives the driver the state to keep in world.json, and `create`
gets it back after a restart, as `saved`. A new lever gets None.

Text.act shows one line to the one who pulls and another to everyone
else in the room.

Try it:

  1. /copy lever. It also makes one of your lever here.
  2. Pull your lever. /edit your lever, at the path /copy gave you, make it
     say something else, then w. Your lever keeps its count: the driver
     moves it onto the new code with its saved state.
  3. Add a second verb, push, that counts down.
