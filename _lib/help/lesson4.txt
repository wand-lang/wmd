Lesson 4: a door that closes itself, with call-outs

Go to the Door Room: west from the Commons, then four. Open the door and
go through. Wait 30 seconds, and it shuts.

Opening the door gives back one more action:

  Driver.After 30s "close"

This is a call-out. 30 seconds later the driver runs the door's own verb
close, with the door as the actor. Call-outs are saved, so they run even
after a restart. /examine door shows the ones waiting.

The room cannot open or close the door; only the door changes itself.
The room's exit asks the door through its props:

  props = Some (fn _ -> {open = JSON.of_bool s.open})

and the exit's check reads it with ctx.world.prop.

Try it:

  1. /copy door. It also makes one of your door here.
  2. Make it close after 10s, then add a verb knock.
