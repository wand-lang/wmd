Lesson 3: a cat that wanders, with heartbeats and Random

Go to the Cat Room: west from the Commons, then three. Wait a while. The
cat may be out; it wanders the Learning Hall.

An object with a heartbeat is called every 2 seconds:

  heartbeat = Some (fn ctx -> ...)

The cat picks a number with Random.int, and `match` decides what the
number means:

  match Random.int 0 29 with
  | 0 -> Driver.Reply(actions = [Driver.Say "The grey cat yawns."])
  | 2 -> _wander ctx
  | _ -> Driver.nothing

Random is the one effect Library code may perform. A file that tries to
read files or the clock is refused when you save it.

To leave, the cat gives back three actions: a Say heard where it is, a
Move, and a Say heard where it arrives. The driver applies them in order.

A heartbeat costs Inspiration every time it runs, paid by the owner. When
you run out, your heartbeats wait until it refills.

Try it:

  1. /copy cat
  2. /eval [Driver.Spawn "/r/home/<you>/cat" ctx.here]
  3. Make it a dog: change its names, its text and what it does.
