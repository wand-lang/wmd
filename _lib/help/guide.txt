The wmd Programmer's Guide

Everything in this world is a Wand program, and you can write your own.
The lessons are rooms in the Learning Hall, west of the Commons. Each one
has a working example to try, and a /help topic that explains it.

  /help lesson1   a room, and text that changes
  /help lesson2   a lever that counts: state and save
  /help lesson3   a cat that wanders: heartbeats and Random
  /help lesson4   a door that closes itself: call-outs
  /help lesson5   a room that makes its own orc: Init and Spawn
  /help lesson6   a dark cave and a lamp: props

How each lesson works:

  1. Go to its room, try the example, and read about it.
  2. /copy the example. That writes its file into your home, as
     /r/home/<you>/lever, and loads it. For a thing, it also makes one here.
  3. /edit your copy, change it, and save it with w. Your change loads at
     once. Type h in the editor for its commands.
  4. For a room, go in with the /move line /copy gives you. For a thing,
     /spawn makes more of it.

Your home is a directory, /r/home/<you>/, and you can make directories in
it to keep your rooms in order, as /r/home/<you>/shop/. /edit and /dig make
any directory a path needs, and /files lists what you have.

To build your own rooms, /dig from where you stand. In the Commons,
/dig hall hall makes your first room, /r/home/<you>/hall. In a room of
yours, /dig east shop/front makes a room in the directory shop and an exit
east to it, and the new room has an exit back. Go through the exit and
/edit here to describe the new room. When you look at a room you may
edit, the last line says which file it is.

Tools that help:

  /verbs <thing>     what a thing answers to
  /examine <thing>   what the driver knows of it: owner, props, call-outs
  /move <path>       go to a room by its path
  /spawn <path>      make one of a thing here
  /eval <expr>       run one line of Wand, as your own body
  /stat me           your Capacity and Inspiration

Building costs Inspiration, which refills over time; /stat me shows how
much you have. Everything you build must follow the rules: /help rules.
