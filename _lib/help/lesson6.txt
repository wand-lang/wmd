Lesson 6: a dark cave and a lamp, with props

Go to the Dark Cave: west from the Commons, then six. It is dark. Light
the lamp, look, and wait.

A lit lamp has a heartbeat that burns its oil. An unlit lamp has none:

  heartbeat = if s.lit then Some (fn _ -> burn s) else None

so it costs nothing until someone lights it. When the oil runs out it
flickers, and a call-out puts it out 4 seconds later. fill lamp fills it.

The lamp shows how much light it gives with props, and the cave adds up
the light of everything in it:

  List.fold_left (fn n id -> n + _light ctx id) 0 (ctx.world.contents ctx.self)

Objects can look at each other with ctx.world.prop, but only change
themselves. /examine lamp shows the props the cave reads.

Try it:

  1. /copy here and /copy lamp
  2. Change your cave to spawn your lamp, as in lesson 5, and /move to it.
  3. Make a second light: a candle that gives light 1 and never runs out.
     Two lit things make the cave brighter; change its text for that.
