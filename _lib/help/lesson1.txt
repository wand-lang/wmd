Lesson 1: a room, and text that changes

Go to the Room of Hours: west from the Commons, then one. Look at it now,
and again in an hour.

A room is an Object, made in a file that starts with
"implement Driver.Blueprint". Its `short` is its name and its `long` is
what look shows. Both are functions that get a Context, `ctx`.

Text that never changes needs no function of your own:

  short = Room.text "Lesson 1: The Room of Hours"

Text that changes is a function. The Room of Hours reads the time from
ctx.time, and puts values into a string with %{...}:

  long = fn ctx -> "The clock says %{DateTime.time_string ctx.time}."

Exits are a map from a name to a place:

  exits = {hall = Room.exit "/r/learning/hall"}

Try it:

  1. /copy here
  2. /move to your copy. /copy tells you its path.
  3. /edit here, find the long text with ,n, change it with c, then w.
  4. look. Your room says what you wrote.

Your room is private: only you, the people you /invite, and the Keymasters
may come in. /public here opens it to everyone.
