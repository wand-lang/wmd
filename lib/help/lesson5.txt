Lesson 5: a room that makes its own orc, with Init and Spawn

Go to the Orc Room: west from the Commons, then five. Poke the orc.

`create` gives back an Init: the object, and actions to apply once it
exists. A new Orc Room asks for an orc:

  | None -> Core.Init(object = orcs, actions = [Core.Spawn "/r/learning/orc" self])
  | Some _ -> Core.Init(object = orcs)

A room brought back after a restart gets its saved state, and spawns
nothing, because its orc was saved too. Without that check, every restart
would add one more orc.

Each Spawn costs Inspiration, and what you own counts against your
Capacity: /stat me shows both.

Try it:

  1. /copy here and /copy orc
  2. /edit /r/home/<you>/orcs, change the Spawn to "/r/home/<you>/orc", then w.
  3. /eval [Core.Move ctx.actor "/r/home/<you>/orcs"]. Your room is made the
     first time you go in, and it spawns your orc. /reset here makes it
     again, as new, and it spawns one more.
