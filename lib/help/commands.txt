Commands that start with / are the driver's, and work anywhere:

  /who [name]          who is awake, or one person
  /help [topic]        this help, or a topic
  /examine [thing]     what the driver knows of a thing or this room
  /stat me             your Capacity and Inspiration
  /edit [path]         edit a file in your home, as /edit /home/ann/lamp
  /update [path]       load a file's new code
  /reset [thing]       make a thing again from its file
  /eval <expression>   run Wand, as your own body
  /sethome             make this room your home
  /public, /private    open or close the room you own
  /invite, /uninvite   let one builder into your private room, or not
  /lock, /unlock       whether others may move a thing you own
  /flag, /report       tell the admins something breaks the rules
  /quit                leave

The rest are your verbs and the room's: look, go, say, pose, home, and
the exits by name.
