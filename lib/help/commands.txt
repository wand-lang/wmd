Commands that start with / are the driver's. They work anywhere a realm
has not turned them off:

  /who [name]          who is awake, or one person
  /help [topic]        this help, or a topic
  /examine [thing]     what the driver knows of a thing or this room
  /stat me             your Capacity and Inspiration
  /verbs [thing]       what a thing, or this room, answers to
  /edit [path]         edit a file in your home, as /edit /r/home/ann/lamp
  /copy <thing>        copy an example from the Learning Hall into your home
  /update [path]       load a file's new code
  /reset [thing]       make a thing again from its file
  /eval <expression>   run Wand, as your own body
  /sethome             make this room your home
  /public, /private    open or close the room you own
  /invite, /uninvite   let one Citizen into your private room, or not
  /ban, /unban         keep one person out of all your rooms, or not
  /lock, /unlock       whether others may move a thing you own
  /flag, /report       tell the Keymasters something breaks the rules
  /quit                leave

The rest are verbs: your own, such as say, pose and home; the room's and
its things'; and the realm's, such as look and go. An exit works by its
name. /verbs here lists the room's and the realm's.

New to building? /help guide starts the lessons.
