The rules of WMD, version 1.

WMD is for everyone 13 and older, and everything here must suit the
youngest of them. Public rooms, objects, descriptions and speech must not
have:

1. Sexual content or innuendo.
2. Graphic violence, gore or cruelty. Cartoon peril is fine: a dragon can
   breathe fire, and a trap can drop you in a pit.
3. Hate or harassment: attacking or mocking anyone for who they are,
   bullying, or targeting a person.
4. Encouraging self-harm, dangerous stunts, or drug and alcohol use.
5. Profanity. Building around a word filter is still a break of this rule.
6. Personal information: real names, addresses, schools, phone numbers,
   social accounts or photos, yours or anyone else's, and asking others
   for theirs.
7. Arranging to meet or talk off WMD, especially with someone younger.
8. Scams, spam, pretending to be another Citizen or a Keymaster, or copying
   someone's work and calling it yours.
9. Code that exists to annoy others: flooding a room with messages,
   trapping people, or anything meant to get around these rules.

For anything not on the list, ask: would it be fine in a game rated for
everyone, in front of a thirteen-year-old and their parent? Private rooms
follow the same rules. Privacy is for unfinished work, not for exceptions.

If something breaks these rules, flag it: /flag here <why> for the room you
are in, or /flag <thing> <why> for a thing. To report a person, use
/report <name> <why>. A Keymaster, or the Steward who keeps the realm, looks
at every flag. Who flagged is told to no one else.
